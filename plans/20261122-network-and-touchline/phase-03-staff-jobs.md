# Phase 3 — Staff take a job for you

An assistant with skill ≥ 12 can take on one brief (mission) or one client need. He shortlists, pitches under
your agency's name, and his skill drives the quality of his picks. He keeps 25% of the commission; you keep
the rest. A bad pitch costs **your** standing with that club. One delegated job per assistant; a report in the
inbox when he pitches and when it resolves. Shown on Home "In progress".
